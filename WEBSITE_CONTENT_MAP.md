# CASAVEE INTERIORS — Website Content Map & Reference Specification

**Document Role:** Primary source-of-truth reference for business identity, website architecture, supplied copy, visual direction, functional requirements, and technical specifications extracted from the official 20-page PDF website brief.  
**Implementation Status:** Stage 1 (Analysis & Content Mapping only). Website pages and features have not yet been implemented.

---

## A. Project Overview

- **Business Name:** CasaVee Interiors (also styled as `CASAVEE INTERIORS` in brand identity headers and logo lockups; note that the founder's personal flagship home project is styled with a space as `Casa Vee`) *(Pages 1, 2, 5, 9, 17, 20)*.
- **Founder:** Vera Sidika *(Pages 1, 2, 6, 7, 15, 20)*.
- **Website Purpose and Objectives:**
  - Build CasaVee Interiors as an independent luxury design house with an identifiable founder, a distinctive design philosophy, and a portfolio capable of attracting high-value residential and commercial clients in Kenya and internationally *(Page 1)*.
  - Make CasaVee look and feel like an established interior design firm from day one *(Page 1)*.
  - Serve as a digital showroom that immediately establishes luxury, showcases the brand's best interiors, and converts visitors into paying clients / encourages visitors to enquire *(Pages 4, 11)*.
  - Establish CasaVee as a design authority, not simply a company showcasing photographs *(Page 13)*.
- **Brand Positioning:** Luxury interior design studio / independent luxury design house *(Pages 1, 2)*.
  - **Brand Sub-descriptor:** `LUXURY INTERIOR DESIGN & SPACE TRANSFORMATION` *(Page 2)*.
  - **Brand Promise:** "We transform spaces into thoughtfully designed environments that reflect individuality, sophistication and elevated living." *(Pages 2, 3)*.
- **Target Audience:**
  - High-value residential and commercial clients *(Page 1)*.
  - Individuals, families, and businesses seeking luxury interior design, space transformation, or expert design direction *(Pages 7, 8, 17)*.
- **Geographic Market:** Kenya (specifically referencing Kenya and Nairobi in search positioning) and International *(Pages 1, 2, 6, 12, 13, 20)*.
- **Brand Personality:** Sophisticated, elegant, creative, confident *(Page 2)*.
